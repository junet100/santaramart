# Privacy Policy for SantaraMart

Last updated: September 18, 2026

SantaraMart ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application within the Pi Network ecosystem.

### 1. Information We Collect
* **Pi Network Account Data:** Through the Pi SDK integration, we access your basic Pi Network username and public wallet address to facilitate login and secure peer-to-peer (P2P) transactions.
* **Order Information:** When you purchase digital products or goods from local MSMEs (UMKM), we collect necessary delivery details (such as phone numbers or shipping addresses provided during the order process).
* **Transaction Logs:** We record transaction hashes and order statuses to monitor distribution and handle customer service requests.

### 2. How We Use Your Information
* To process your orders for digital assets, vouchers, and MSME products.
* To verify payments made via the Pi Network Testnet/Mainnet wallet.
* To provide reliable customer support regarding delayed deliveries or vendor queues.

### 3. Data Protection and Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Information is only shared with the specific merchant/store you ordered from to facilitate physical shipping or digital product validation.

### 4. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us inside the SantaraMart application framework.
